Stage 01 · Phone screen

The recruiter call

TestsCan you tell your story in numbers, in a few minutes.

Where people fallSelling traits, like hungry and competitive, instead of evidence.

How EffyX preps youTurns your experience into numbers: the target, how much you hit, and for how long. Then rehearses your opening.

Stage 02 · Home assignment

The task

TestsProof of skill: a presentation, a case or a role-play, on a deadline.

Where people fallDelivery under pressure, and hours spent without knowing what the task measures.

How EffyX preps youDecodes what the task really tests, then rehearses the delivery with the questions you didn't plan for.

Stage 03 · Hiring manager + HR

The table

TestsAttitude, credibility and how you solve problems.

Where people fallOne wrong word, like criticizing a past manager, or a defensive answer about weaknesses.

How EffyX preps youTrains the trap questions and builds a bank of your own problem-solving stories.

Stage 04 · VP or CEO

The big picture

TestsFit with the company's vision.

How EffyX preps youWrites you a brief on the company and your answer to why this company and why this role.

Stage 05 · References

The check

TestsThat the numbers you gave check out.

Where people fallA claim a reference can't confirm.

How EffyX preps youNever invents experience. What you claim is what your references can confirm.

Stage 06 · Contract

The offer

TestsNegotiation: base, commission and OTE.

Where people fallNaming a number first, or accepting without asking.

How EffyX preps youDebriefs every stage with you, then rehearses how to frame the offer conversation.

Is it a person or AI?

No. It builds the preparation and the structure around every interview, so you walk in ready on your own.

What happens to my CV?

The agent reads it, fixes it and gets it ready to send. Every change is saved, so you can see what changed and edit it yourself.

Yes, on purpose. EffyX is built for people who want to win in sales: SDR, account executive, account manager and sales leadership. Everything it knows is about how high-tech hires salespeople.
