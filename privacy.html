Privacy Policy

Last updated · June 2026

EffyX ("we", "us") is currently in early access. This page explains what information we collect through this waitlist site, and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, and role, when you request access.
  • Resume content: if you use the resume-review tool, the resume you upload (PDF or text) and the role you select.
  • Usage data: if analytics is enabled, anonymous, aggregated page-view data (e.g. via Plausible, which is cookie-free and doesn't track you across other sites).

How we use it

  • To notify you when early access opens.
  • To understand demand by role, so we prioritize the right capabilities first.
  • To generate your resume review, and to contact you about EffyX.

Where it's stored

Your name, email, and role are stored in a private Google Sheet via a Google Apps Script integration. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Resume review & AI processing

When you use the resume-review tool, the resume you upload (PDF or text) and the email and role you provide are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), which generates your review. We use this content only to produce your review and to contact you about EffyX — we don't sell it, and Anthropic does not train its models on data submitted through its API. We don't keep a copy of the uploaded file after your review is generated; your name, email, and role are kept as described above.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your information at any time by emailing barmenashe59@gmail.com. We'll action requests promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as EffyX moves from waitlist to product. Material changes will be reflected with a new "last updated" date above.
